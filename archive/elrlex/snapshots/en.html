The Firm

ELR LEX Avvocati Associati (“ERL LEX”) is a young, dynamic and independent firm formed in 2014 by a group of lawyers who had previously worked together for some time at prestigious law firms, serving important Italian and international clients.

Our Experience

The founding partners of ELR LEX are convinced that their more than 30 years of collective experience, combined with their international, business-oriented approach to the practice of law, allow ELR LEX to provide its clients distinctive, particularly effective and highly qualified counsel and advice.
ELR LEX pursues the following fundamental values in its practice: independence, integrity, professionalism, quality of legal counsel, a pragmatic approach, proximity to its clients and efficiency. ELR LEX has a streamlined, efficient structure that allows its partners to enjoy close working relationships with clients, meeting their demands and also providing them with custom-tailored solutions highly suited to their specific needs.
ELR LEX also has access to a group of young professionals and consultants to ensure a more comprehensive approach to each case.

Areas of Interest

The legal counsel and advice provided by ELR LEX extends to almost all areas of interest for companies, and most importantly:
- M&A and corporate law
- Capital markets
- Banking and financial law
- Funds
- Restructuring and bankruptcy procedures
- Real-estate transactions
- Renewable energy
- Contracts
- Litigation and arbitration

Our Clients

The clients to whom the lawyers at ELR LEX provide their advice and counsel include leading Italian and international industrial groups, Italian banking groups, alternative investment managers, investment funds, financial institutions, insurance companies and pharmaceutical companies.
